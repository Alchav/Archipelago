# Spicy Mycena 64

## Where is the options page?

The player options page for this game contains all the options you need to configure and export a config file. Player
options page link: [SM64EX Player Options Page](../player-options).

## What does randomization do to this game?
Power Stars are location checks, but this fork does not generate Power Star items. Progression comes from castle keys,
cap items, movement items if enabled, and feature items that enable specific level objects such as bosses, races,
buddies, MIPS, Toads, Courtyard Boos, the Wing Cap light, and the castle cannon. Cap items can be either global or
per-level, depending on the Per-Level Cap Items option.


## What is the goal of SM64EX when randomized?
As in most Mario games, save the Princess!

## Which items can be in another player's world?
The progression item pool includes castle keys, cap items, optional cannon unlocks and movement items, and the SM64
feature items. Power Star items are kept only as an old client compatibility ID and are not generated by this fork.

## What does another world's item look like in SM64EX?
The items are visually unchanged, though after collecting a message will pop up to inform you what you collected,
and who will receive it.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?
When you receive an item, a message will pop up to inform you where you received the item from,
and which one it is.

NOTE: The Secret Star count in the menu is broken.
